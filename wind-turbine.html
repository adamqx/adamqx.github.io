3-D printed wind turbine

Design project

The aim was usable electrical output from a wind turbine whose mechanical structure is fully 3-D printed, with the wiring built in.

What I did

  • Designed the turbine in Siemens NX CAD.
  • Printed the components in ABS and built the prototype.
  • Targeted usable electrical output, with the wiring integrated into the printed structure.

Tools

Siemens NX, 3-D printing (QIDI Pro), ABS.
