Electro-drilling for Arctic oil extraction

Research project

Electro-drilling breaks rock with high-voltage energy and electrothermal heating instead of only mechanical force. This project studied whether it could improve rock penetration and fit an Arctic drilling plan.

What I did

  • Researched electro-drilling using high-voltage energy and electrothermal heating to improve rock penetration.
  • Developed an Arctic drilling framework that combines directional drilling, real-time monitoring, enhanced oil recovery, and cold-environment equipment protection.
  • Examined well integrity, storage, and environmental controls.

Tools

Literature review, systems framework design.
