Custom gaming tower

Personal project

A custom computer and enclosure built mostly from parts recovered from obsolete systems.

What I did

  • Built the enclosure from vehicular carbon-alloy segments and plastic components.
  • Used an AMD Ryzen 5 CPU, NVIDIA RTX 4060 GPU, 1 TB SSD, and 32 GB RAM.
  • Bought only the CPU and GPU, and recovered every other part from obsolete systems.

PC hardware, fabrication, troubleshooting.
