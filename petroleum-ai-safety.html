AI integration for petroleum industry safety

Research project

Major industrial disasters in the petroleum industry often trace back to human error. This project looked at how that happens and whether AI could help people in high-risk drilling environments make better decisions.

What I did

  • Analyzed major industrial disasters to understand the role of human error in petroleum operations.
  • Proposed AI-based decision-support systems for high-risk drilling environments.
  • Evaluated AI for equipment monitoring, risk mitigation, and operational awareness.
  • Kept engineers as the final decision-makers in every proposed system.

Tools

Case-study analysis, systems design, literature review.
