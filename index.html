My research looks at how failures happen in high-risk settings, from drilling operations to impact sensing, and how AI can support the people making the final call. I’m a member of ASME, a Hicks Honors College student, and on the Dean’s List.

Research

AI integration for petroleum industry safety

I studied major industrial disasters to understand how human error shows up in petroleum operations, then proposed AI-based decision-support systems for high-risk drilling environments. I evaluated AI for equipment monitoring, risk mitigation, and operational awareness, with engineers kept as the final decision-makers.

Electro-drilling for Arctic oil extraction

I researched electro-drilling, which uses high-voltage energy and electrothermal heating to improve rock penetration. I developed an Arctic drilling framework that combines directional drilling, real-time monitoring, enhanced oil recovery, and cold-environment equipment protection, and I examined well integrity, storage, and environmental controls.

Inertial Fluidic Acceleration Sensors (IFAS)

I designed a low-cost passive acceleration sensor that uses fluid inertia to rupture a thin gold membrane during high-impact events. I ran quantitative analysis and finite element analysis (FEA) on how membrane thickness, fluid volume, and sensor geometry affect rupture initiation at 100g, and investigated working-fluid properties and capillary effects to set dimensions for prototyping.

Projects

Thermoelectric refrigeration system

I built a refrigeration apparatus from a Peltier module, Styrofoam insulation, heat sinks, and circuitry, under rules that ruled out liquid cooling and pressurized systems. I calculated coefficient of performance, entropy generation, irreversibility, and heat-transfer rates in Excel, and proposed better insulation, more heat exchange, and multi-module designs.

3-D printed wind turbine

I designed the turbine in Siemens NX and printed it in ABS, aiming for usable electrical output from a fully 3-D-printed mechanical structure with integrated wiring.

Custom gaming tower

I built a computer and enclosure from vehicular carbon-alloy segments and plastic components, with an AMD Ryzen 5 CPU, NVIDIA RTX 4060 GPU, 1 TB SSD, and 32 GB RAM. I bought only the CPU and GPU and recovered every other part from obsolete systems.
